How to play

Everything the game does, in one page.

The run

A run lasts thirty-three seconds. Objects arrive from all over the screen. Tap one to smash it.

The rule that matters

Hits in a row build a multiplier: five in a row takes you to ×2, twelve to ×3, twenty-five to ×4 and fifty to ×5.

A miss drops you straight back to ×1. A miss is any tap that lands on nothing. This is why tapping faster is not automatically better — the targets are deliberately small, and precision is what pays.

Clocks and bombs

A clock adds eight seconds to the timer. A bomb clears everything near it — saved for a crowded screen, one bomb is the biggest single scoring move in the game.

Themes

Six of them, chosen from THEMES on the front screen: Classic, Neon, Beach, Western, Planet and Insect. Each has its own objects, colours and sounds. A theme you have not looked at yet carries a NEW badge.

Awards

Six, and all but one can be earned again and again — the trophy case keeps count.

  • Double Five — Reach ×5 twice in one run
  • Time Hoarder — Collect five clocks in one run
  • Big Bang — Clear fifteen objects with one bomb
  • Flawless — Finish a run without a single miss
  • High Roller — Score 2,000 in one run
  • Grand Tour — Play a run on every theme

Between your devices

Your best score and your awards sync over iCloud, so a trophy earned on an iPad shows up on your iPhone.

A few things that help

  • Let a crowd build before you start chaining — misses are cheap when the screen is empty and expensive when it isn't.
  • A bomb in a full screen beats any single box, by a lot.
  • When the timer turns red you have five seconds left. A clock is worth more than a red box at that point.
