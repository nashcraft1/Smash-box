Smash Box

Thirty-three seconds. One rule: don't miss.

Boxes arrive from every direction. Tap them to smash them before the clock runs out — but a tap that hits nothing breaks your chain and costs you more than the point you missed.

Six themes, six awards, and a best score that follows you between your iPhone and iPad. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, and nothing collected about you.

How to play

  1. 1

    Tap the boxes

    Boxes fly in from every side. Tap one to smash it and score its points.

  2. 2

    Chain to ×5

    Hits in a row raise your multiplier — ×2 at five, up to ×5 at fifty. A tap that hits nothing sends you back to ×1.

  3. 3

    Grab clocks, save bombs

    A clock adds eight seconds. A bomb clears everything around it — best when the screen is crowded.
