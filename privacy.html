Privacy Policy

Smash Box does not collect anything.

There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests to us, because there is no server on our side for it to talk to.

What the app stores

Your best score, your awards, your chosen theme and your haptics setting are saved on your device by the app. That is the entire list.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your best score and your awards are copied to Apple's iCloud key-value store so that they follow you between your own devices. That data sits in your iCloud account, under your Apple ID. We cannot see it and we have no way to reach it. Turning iCloud off for Smash Box in iOS Settings stops this; the game carries on working exactly as before, just on that device alone.

Children

Smash Box collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Removing your data

Deleting the app removes everything it saved on that device. To remove the synced copy too, turn iCloud off for Smash Box in iOS Settings before deleting it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
